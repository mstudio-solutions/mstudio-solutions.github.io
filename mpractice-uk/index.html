Common questions

Are these the real test questions?

No. The questions are based on Life in the United Kingdom: A Guide for New Residents (3rd edition), the official handbook. They are not the real test questions. All explanations are written in our own words.

Is this an official government app?

No. mPractice is an unofficial practice tool. It is not made by, linked to or approved by the UK Government or the Home Office. Always check GOV.UK for the latest rules.

Why is an answer different from today's facts?

The test still uses the 3rd edition handbook, and some facts in it are out of date, for example the monarch, the population and the jury age. In these questions, the explanation gives you both the handbook answer and today's fact.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. All questions are built into the app, so it works fully offline.

How do I clear my scores?

Tap Clear under Recent Sessions. Deleting the app also removes all your scores and progress.

How does the free version work?

You get 1 practice session a day, in any mode. The session is used when you answer the first question. A new free session is ready the next day.

What do I get when I unlock?

Unlimited practice, every day, in every mode. You pay once. There is no subscription.

I paid, but the app is still locked

Tap the About button (the globe at the top right), then tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you paid with. You can also find it on the unlock screen. This also works on a new iPhone or after you delete the app.

Can I get a refund?

Payments and refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and choose the purchase.

Can I change the language?

Yes. Tap the About button (the globe at the top right) and choose a language at the top of the page. The menus change. The questions and answers stay in English.

Does mPractice collect my data?

No. See the Privacy Policy.
