mPractice Canada Citizenship

Practise for the Canadian citizenship test.

What it does

  • 501 practice questions based on the study guide Discover Canada
  • Full mock test: 20 questions, 15 correct (75%) to pass, like the real test
  • Practise by topic, key questions or random
  • Multiple choice and True/False questions
  • A short explanation for every question
  • Timer per question, or no timer
  • Large buttons and clear text
  • Works offline, with no ads and no account

Common questions

Are these the real test questions?

No. The questions are based on Discover Canada: The Rights and Responsibilities of Citizenship, the official study guide. They are not the real test questions.

Is this an official government app?

No. mPractice is an unofficial practice tool. It is not made by, linked to or approved by the Government of Canada or Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC). Always check canada.ca for the latest rules.

Why is an answer different from my copy of the guide?

Some facts in older copies of the guide are out of date, for example King Charles III, the 2021 oath and the 343 electoral districts. In these questions, the explanation gives you both today's fact and what the guide says.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. All questions are built into the app, so it works fully offline.

How do I clear my scores?

Tap Clear under Recent Sessions. Deleting the app also removes all your scores and progress.

Does mPractice collect my data?

No. See the Privacy Policy.
