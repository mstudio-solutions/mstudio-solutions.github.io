mPractice Canadian Citizenship Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Summary

mPractice does not collect, store or share any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no ads and no tracking.

Information we collect

None. mPractice does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal details. All questions are built into the app, and your answers and scores are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Data on your device

Your settings, recent scores and the questions you have already done are saved only on your iPhone, so you can carry on next time. This data never leaves your device. You can remove it with the Clear button under Recent Sessions, or by deleting the app.

Ads and tracking

mPractice has no ads, no analytics and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).

Purchases

Unlimited practice is a one-time in-app purchase. Payment is handled by Apple. We never see your name, your Apple Account or your payment details. Apple's own privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Links to other sites

The app links to the Government of Canada website (canada.ca). Their own privacy policy applies when you visit it.

Children

mPractice is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be shown on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email mstudiosolutions@gmail.com. mStudio Solutions, Sydney, Australia.
