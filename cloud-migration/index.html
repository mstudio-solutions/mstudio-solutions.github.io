Cloud migration

Move your website or server to the cloud.

What we do

We help you move your website, web app or server to Microsoft Azure, for example to Azure App Service. We plan the move, do the work and check that everything runs well after.

Why move to the cloud

Platform as a Service (PaaS)

Your website runs on a platform on Microsoft Azure, not on your own server. Azure looks after the servers, the network and the hardware. You only look after your website.

Always up to date

The platform always runs on an up-to-date operating system, with the latest security fixes. Azure does the updates for you, so you don't need to worry about patching.

Scales with your business

Your website can scale by itself, based on how busy your business is. When more people visit, it adds more power. When it is quiet, it scales back down.

Focus on your business

We set up the platform to fit your website and how you use it. You don't need to guess what you need or spend time on servers. You can focus on your business.
