mPath Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Summary

mPath does not collect, store or share any personal information. It does not connect to the internet. There is no account, no ads and no tracking.

Information we collect

None. The folders and paths you type stay on your Mac and are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Permissions

mPath asks for Accessibility, to place the bar on your Finder window, and for permission to control Finder, to open the folder you type. mPath uses these only on your Mac.

Data on your Mac

Your mPath settings are saved only on your Mac. If you turn on the troubleshooting log, it is saved only on your Mac too. Uninstalling mPath removes the app.

Children

mPath is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be shown on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email mstudiosolutions@gmail.com. mStudio Solutions, Sydney, Australia.
