# mstudio-solutions.github.io

Website for mStudio Solutions (https://mstudiosolutions.com): app support pages and privacy policies.

- `/` home
- `/about/` About us
- `/contact/` contact form (sends email; see WEBSITE.md)
- `/mcurrency/` mCurrency support (App Store Support URL)
- `/mcurrency/privacy/` mCurrency privacy policy (App Store Privacy Policy URL)
- `/mpractice/` mPractice Citizenship Test support (App Store Support URL)
- `/mpractice/privacy/` mPractice Citizenship Test privacy policy (App Store Privacy Policy URL)
- `/mpractice-canada/`, `/mpractice-uk/`, `/mpractice-aussie/` (and their `/privacy/`) only send visitors to `/mpractice/`. The three old apps were joined into mPractice Citizenship Test.
- `/iac-bicep-generator/` IaC Generator for Bicep details (free web tool)
- `/mtranslate/`, `/mtranslate-chrome/`, `/mmarkdown/`, `/clean-microsoft-teams-accounts/` other app support pages
- `/cloud-migration/` Cloud migration service
- `/mpath/` mPath support (macOS app, Mac App Store)
- `/mpath/privacy/` mPath privacy policy
- `/config/mcurrency.json` forced-update settings for mCurrency.
  Raise `minVersion` to force users on older versions to update.
  After launch, set `storeUrl` to the App Store link.
