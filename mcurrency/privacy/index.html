mCurrency Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Summary

mCurrency does not collect, store or share any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no ads and no tracking.

Information we collect

None. mCurrency does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal details.

Data on your device

Your settings (currency list, language, theme) and the latest rates are saved only on your iPhone so the app works offline. This data never leaves your device and is removed if you delete the app.

Exchange rate services

To get the latest rates, the app connects to public rate services: Frankfurter, Currency-API (for gold and silver) and ExchangeRate-API as a backup. Like any website, these services can see your IP address when the app asks for rates. No personal information is sent to them.

Ads and tracking

mCurrency has no ads, no analytics and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).

Children

mCurrency is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be shown in the app and on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email mstudiosolutions@gmail.com. mStudio, Sydney, Australia.
