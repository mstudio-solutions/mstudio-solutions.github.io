mTranslate

Translate and hear any text on your Mac, with one shortcut.

What it does

  • Copy text in any app, press Control-Option-T, and see the translation next to your mouse
  • Press the speaker button to hear the text or the translation
  • Cantonese or Mandarin voice for Chinese
  • A big window to type, paste and swap languages
  • Also in the right-click menu: Services → Translate with mTranslate
  • Free Chrome extension: select a word on any web page and see its translation in a yellow bubble
  • Uses Apple's built-in translation, works offline
  • Free, with no ads and no account

mTranslate for Chrome

A free extension for Google Chrome. It works on its own, with or without the Mac app.

  • Select text or double-click a word on a web page. A yellow bubble shows the translation.
  • Single words also show the whole sentence they're in, so you see which meaning is meant
  • Press the speaker to hear it, with a Cantonese or Mandarin voice for Chinese
  • Or select text, right-click, and choose mTranslate
  • Press the mTranslate icon in Chrome's toolbar to type or paste text
  • Uses Chrome's built-in translation on your computer. Your text isn't sent anywhere.

Download mTranslate for Chrome

How to install

  1. Click Download mTranslate for Chrome.
  2. Open mTranslate-for-Chrome-1.2.3.zip from your Downloads folder. You get a folder called mTranslate for Chrome.
  3. Move that folder to a place where it will stay, for example Documents. Chrome uses it from there, so don't delete it.
  4. In Chrome, type chrome://extensions in the address bar and press Return.
  5. Turn on Developer mode (top right).
  6. Press Load unpacked (top left). Open the mTranslate for Chrome folder and press Select. Make sure nothing inside the folder is selected.
  7. Press the puzzle icon in Chrome's toolbar, then the pin next to mTranslate.

Now select a word on any web page. Pages that were already open need a reload first.

Settings

Press Settings in the yellow bubble, or right-click the mTranslate icon in the toolbar and choose Options. You can choose:

  • Your language. The bubble also uses it for its own words.
  • What happens when you select text: show the translation right away, show a small icon to click, or nothing
  • A Cantonese or Mandarin voice for Chinese
  • Whether single words also show the whole sentence

Update to a new version

Download the new file, replace the old mTranslate for Chrome folder with the new one, then press the reload button on the mTranslate card in chrome://extensions.

Common questions

How do I translate something?

Select the text and press Command-C to copy it. Then press Control-Option-T. A small bubble opens next to your mouse. Press Esc or click anywhere else to close it.

Why do I have to copy the text first?

Mac App Store apps are not allowed to read what you select in other apps. Copying the text gives it to mTranslate. Or select the text, right-click, and choose Services → Translate with mTranslate.

Why isn't mTranslate for Chrome in the Chrome Web Store?

Not yet. Until it is, install it from the file on this page with Developer mode, as shown above. Chrome may remind you that developer mode extensions are on. That's normal for extensions installed this way.

Chrome says it's downloading a language. Why?

Chrome translates on your computer, so it needs each language once. After that it's quick and works without internet.

The Mac app asks me to download a language. Why?

Translation happens on your Mac, so macOS needs each language once. After the download it works without internet.

How do I change the voice or the languages?

Open mTranslate and go to Settings (the gear in the window, or mTranslate → Settings in the menu bar). Choose your language, the language you translate into, and a Cantonese or Mandarin voice for Chinese.

The voice sounds robotic. Can I make it better?

Yes. In macOS System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → System Voice → Manage Voices, download an Enhanced or Premium voice. mTranslate uses the best voice you have.

How do I start mTranslate when I log in?

Turn on Open mTranslate at login in Settings.

Does mTranslate collect my data?

No. Your text never leaves your Mac. See the Privacy Policy.
