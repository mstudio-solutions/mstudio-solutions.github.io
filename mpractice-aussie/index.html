Common questions

Are these the real test questions?

No. The questions are based on the testable sections of Australian Citizenship: Our Common Bond, the official book. They are not the real test questions.

Is this an official government app?

No. mPractice is an unofficial practice tool. It is not made by, linked to or approved by the Australian Government or the Department of Home Affairs. Always check the Home Affairs website for the latest rules.

Why did I fail the mock test with 75% or more?

Like the real test, you must get all 5 Australian values questions correct to pass. If you miss one, you do not pass, even with a high score. Use the values-only mode to practise them.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. All questions are built into the app, so it works fully offline.

How do I clear my scores?

Tap Clear under Recent Sessions. Deleting the app also removes all your scores and progress.

Does mPractice collect my data?

No. See the Privacy Policy.
