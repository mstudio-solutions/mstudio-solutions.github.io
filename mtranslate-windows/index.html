mTranslate for Windows

A powerful translator that's easy to use. Copy any text, open mTranslate, and the translation is ready.

Coming soon to the Microsoft Store.

What it does

  • Copy text in any app, then open mTranslate. The text is pasted in and translated straight away
  • Works with the mouse alone: right-click, Copy, then click mTranslate on the taskbar
  • Your text on the left, the translation on the right. mTranslate finds the language for you
  • Press the speaker button to hear the text or the translation, with the voices on your PC
  • Cantonese or Mandarin voice for Chinese
  • Press Clear to start again with new text
  • Text size: small, medium, large or extra large
  • Translates on your PC and works offline
  • Free, with no ads and no account. Buy me a coffee is an optional tip and unlocks nothing

Use a Mac? See mTranslate for Mac. Use Chrome? See mTranslate for Chrome.

Common questions

How do I translate something?

Select the text and copy it (right-click and choose Copy, or press Ctrl+C). Then open mTranslate, or click it on the taskbar. The text is pasted in and translated. You can also type or paste text yourself.

Why do I have to copy the text first?

mTranslate translates the text you copy. Selecting text without copying it is not enough.

It asks me to download a language. Why?

Translation happens on your PC, so mTranslate needs each language once. After the download it works without internet.

How do I hear a Cantonese voice?

mTranslate uses the voices in Windows. To add one, open Windows Settings → Time & language → Speech → Add voices and add Chinese (Traditional, Hong Kong).

What is Buy me a coffee?

An optional tip to support the app. It does not unlock anything; every feature is free. Payment is handled by the Microsoft Store.

Does mTranslate collect my data?

No. Your text never leaves your PC. See the Privacy Policy.
